Generate AI Quizzes from Any Topic on Telegram

You don't always need a PDF or lecture photos to test yourself. With Topic Quiz (/topic) in Abiad Bot on Telegram, type a topic title or send a short voice note, and the bot generates 4-option multiple-choice questions with hints and explanations.

Have a PDF or notes file instead? Check out Convert PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions or the Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

Try Topic Quiz How It Works

How to Create a Quiz from Any Topic

  1. Open Topic Quiz

    Tap «🎯 Topic Quiz» in the bot's main menu, or send the /topic command directly.

  2. Type Your Topic or Send a Voice Note

    Write a concise description (3 to 500 characters) such as «Newton's Laws of Motion — intermediate level», or record a short voice message (up to 60 seconds).

  3. Choose Count & Difficulty

    The bot automatically extracts the topic, subject, and language, letting you pick question count, style, and difficulty before confirming credit cost.

  4. Solve, Practice, or Export

    Answer interactively inside Telegram, drill your mistakes, or export to Word or PDF.

Input Limits & Guidelines

Text Prompt
3 to 500 characters. More specific prompts (e.g., grade level or subtopic) produce more focused questions.
Voice Prompt
Short voice message up to 60 seconds and 2 MB max, with a confirmation screen before generation.
Content Safety
Prompts pass through automated moderation with temporary cooldowns for repeated misuse, per our Terms of Service.
Saving Important Quizzes
Text-generated quizzes not saved in Favorites expire automatically after inactivity; bookmark important quizzes to keep them permanently.

Frequently Asked Questions About Topic Quiz

Do I need a PDF or photo to use Topic Quiz?

No. Simply type a topic title or short description (3 to 500 characters) or send a short voice message up to 60 seconds, and the bot generates a multiple-choice quiz immediately.

How do I start Topic Quiz inside Abiad Bot?

Tap the «Topic Quiz» button in the main menu or send the /topic command, then enter your topic as text or a short voice note.

Which languages are supported for Topic Quiz?

The bot detects the language of your topic prompt and generates questions in that language, while the bot interface supports 6 languages (Arabic, English, Turkish, French, German, and Persian).

Can I export a topic-generated quiz to PDF or Word?

Yes. Once generated, you can save the quiz to Favorites, share a link, or export it as Word or PDF in Student or Teacher edition.
