Create Quizzes from Word or PowerPoint Files

Send your Word (docx), PowerPoint (pptx), or plain text (txt) file to «Abiad» bot on Telegram. The bot parses the text in your file and synthesizes 4-choice multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in interactive quizzes solved directly within Telegram. Images embedded inside slides or documents are not parsed in this pipeline, and legacy doc and ppt formats are not supported.

For general instructions on creating quizzes and exploring other supported formats, read our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

How to Convert Word or PowerPoint into a Quiz

  1. Verify File Format

    Ensure your file is in docx, pptx, or txt format. If your file is in older doc or ppt format, re-save it in the modern format before sending, as explained in the "Have a Legacy doc or ppt File?" section below.

  2. Send the File to the Bot

    Open a chat with the bot and send your file as a document.

  3. Choose Question Count & Settings

    Select anywhere from 1 to 120 questions, and configure difficulty and question types based on your subject. If your material is in English or French, check out English & French Quizzes with Translations.

  4. Solve the Quiz Inside Telegram

    Receive 4-choice multiple-choice questions directly in your chat. Request smart hints as you solve, and review detailed answer explanations.

Have a Legacy doc or ppt File?

Although Telegram allows you to select these legacy file types, the bot cannot parse them; the generation will fail and your credits will be refunded automatically. To resolve this, simply open the file in Microsoft Word or PowerPoint, select "Save As" or "Export" to modern docx or pptx format, and upload the new version.

What If Your Material Relies Heavily on Images?

Images embedded inside Word or PowerPoint files are not parsed in this pipeline and will not be included in questions. You can transcribe image text directly into the document, or send the pages as separate standalone images (or direct text), as described in our Images to Quiz Guide and Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • Images embedded within Word and PowerPoint files are not read in this file pipeline.
  • Legacy doc and ppt formats are unsupported; use docx and pptx instead.
  • Questions are strictly 4-choice multiple-choice questions (MCQs); true/false, short answer, and essay questions are not supported.
  • Questions are generated by AI and may occasionally contain inaccuracies; review answers before important use.
  • Quizzes are taken interactively inside Telegram; they cannot be solved on this website.

Frequently Asked Questions About Word & PowerPoint Quizzes

Does the bot read tables inside Word documents?

Yes. The bot extracts text from tables alongside paragraph text in docx files.

Does the bot parse images embedded in slides or Word files?

No. Embedded images in Word and PowerPoint documents are not parsed in this workflow. For pptx files, only text inside text boxes is read.

What should I do with an older ppt PowerPoint file?

Open it in PowerPoint and re-save it as pptx before sending it to the bot. The same applies to legacy doc files: save them as docx first.

Can I send a plain txt file instead of Word?

Yes, the bot accepts txt files. If your text is short, you can also paste it directly into the chat (supported between 30 and 8,000 characters).
