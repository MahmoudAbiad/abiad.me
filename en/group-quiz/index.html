Run a Group Quiz in Your Telegram Group or Channel

Abiad Bot empowers administrators of Telegram groups and channels to launch interactive quizzes where members participate and compete together in real time. An admin starts the session using the /groupquiz command or via the share button, choosing between a competitive mode featuring an end-of-quiz leaderboard and a completely anonymous practice mode.

This guide is specifically tailored for group admins, channel owners, educators, and study leaders. For general steps on creating a quiz from your study material, read our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

Start on Telegram How Does It Work?

How to Host a Group Quiz

  1. Prepare your quiz

    Generate a quiz from your lesson material in the bot as usual. All questions are standard 4-option multiple-choice. Creation steps are detailed in our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

  2. Start the session from your group or channel

    An administrator launches the quiz by typing the /groupquiz command in the group chat or tapping the share button. Only group or channel administrators possess permission to initiate group quiz sessions.

  3. Configure mode, timer, and question pace

    Choose between competitive or anonymous mode, toggle participant names on or off, configure per-question timers, and choose the delivery pace between questions. Full details in the sections below.

  4. End the session when finished

    Administrators can manually end the active quiz session at any moment using the «End Session» button.

Live Questions Inside Your Group

Quiz questions are delivered to the group sequentially, and members vote directly using Telegram's interactive poll choices. This example showcases real-time vote tallies and the dedicated session control button available to group admins.

Competitive Mode vs. Anonymous Mode

Comparison of Group Quiz Operating Modes
ModeWhat Happens
CompetitiveScores are recorded and a final leaderboard is published ranking the top participants.
Fully AnonymousNo individual scores or user rankings are recorded during this session.

Select competitive mode when you wish to reward top performers and publish a final leaderboard. Anonymous mode is ideal for relaxed review, poll surveys, or low-pressure group practice.

Administrator Controls & Settings

Participant Names
Show or hide participant names alongside their poll answers.
Per-Question Countdown Timer
No timer, 15 seconds, 30 seconds, 45 seconds, or 60 seconds per question.
Question Delivery Pace
Send a question every 30 seconds, every 1 minute, every 2 minutes, every 5 minutes, or automatically when the previous timer expires.
Manual Termination
Admins can stop the session at any point with one click.
Zero Credit Cost
Launching and hosting a group quiz is completely free and deducts zero credits. Generating the initial quiz from your study material consumes credits as usual.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • Group quizzes can only be started by administrators of the respective Telegram group or channel.
  • Anonymous mode does not track scores; leaderboard rankings are strictly available in competitive mode.
  • Questions are 4-option multiple-choice format generated by artificial intelligence; review question wording prior to running live quizzes in front of large audiences.
  • Generating the quiz from your files uses credits (welcome credits and periodic renewals apply; usage is not unlimited). Hosting the live session is completely free.
  • Most general bot commands run inside private 1-on-1 chats; the group quiz engine is specifically engineered for group chats and channels.
  • This tool is designed for educational revision, team practice, and study groups; using it for official proctored examinations or academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Review our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who has permission to launch a group quiz?

Only verified administrators of the Telegram group or channel, using the /groupquiz command or the in-chat share button.

Is there an end-of-session leaderboard in anonymous mode?

No. Anonymous mode does not track individual scores or user identities. Final leaderboards and rankings are exclusive to competitive mode.

Does hosting a group quiz consume user credits?

No. Running and solving a group quiz in your group or channel is completely free and costs zero credits. Only creating the quiz from source files deducts credits.

How can I adjust the speed of questions?

You can set an answer countdown timer (15s, 30s, 45s, 60s, or disabled) and select the delivery interval: every 30 seconds, 1 minute, 2 minutes, 5 minutes, or immediately upon timer expiration.
