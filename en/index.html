Real Example of AI Quiz Generation

We sent the following lesson paragraph to Abiad Bot, requesting 3 comprehensive easy-level questions. Here are the resulting questions after reviewing wording and options:

The Input Paragraph

The Water Cycle: The sun heats water in seas and rivers, turning part of it into water vapor through evaporation. Water vapor rises and cools, condensing into tiny droplets that gather to form clouds. When droplets grow larger and heavier than air can support, they fall to the ground as precipitation like rain. Water collects back in rivers and seas, and part of it evaporates again to sustain the cycle.

Question 1 of 3

Which process turns river and sea water into vapor under solar heat?

  1. Evaporation
  2. Melting
  3. Sedimentation
  4. Condensation
Show answer & explanation

Answer: Evaporation. The text explicitly states that solar heating converts water into vapor through the evaporation process.

Question 2 of 3

What causes cloud droplets to fall as rain?

  1. Wind speeds increase
  2. Droplet weight exceeds air support
  3. Air temperature rises
  4. Air humidity drops
Show answer & explanation

Answer: Droplet weight exceeds air support. Droplets fall when they grow large and heavy enough that air currents can no longer hold them up.

Question 3 of 3

Which of the following physical changes was NOT mentioned in the text?

  1. Condensation
  2. Precipitation
  3. Evaporation
  4. Melting
Show answer & explanation

Answer: Melting. Evaporation, condensation, and precipitation were directly mentioned. Melting (solid to liquid transition) was not in the text.
