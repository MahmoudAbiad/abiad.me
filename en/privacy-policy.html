Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the Abiad service handles data and content when using the Abiad Telegram bot, the abiad.me website, and associated pages.

We collect and process data required to operate the service for the purposes described in this policy. By using Abiad, you acknowledge having reviewed this policy. If a consent screen appears in the bot, your acceptance constitutes agreement to data processing as outlined herein.

1.What is the Abiad Service?

Abiad is an educational service that helps users create quizzes and questions from text, files, images, and audio recordings using software tools and artificial intelligence technologies.

The service comprises the Telegram bot, the informational website, and associated web tools and pages.

2.Data We May Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Telegram account details provided by Telegram to the bot, such as numeric user ID, first/last name, and username if available.
  • Files, images, text snippets, and audio recordings you choose to submit to the service.
  • Edits you make to generated questions or answers.
  • Usage data related to the service, such as quizzes created or solved, scores, saved favorites, referrals, credit balances, activity logs, and incorrect answers required for review and targeted practice.
  • Information you voluntarily submit to technical support or within feedback and reports.
  • Wallet and gift records, including free and purchased balances, redeemed gift codes, source, value, grant dates, expiry dates, and consumption history.
  • Information required to verify credit top-ups, such as package selected, payment method, order code, status, transaction ID, or transfer receipts when needed. This may be matched with payment notifications from the payment provider.
  • Limited technical data that assists us in detecting bugs, securing the service, and preventing abuse.

We do not deliberately solicit data not required to deliver the service. The website displays two aggregate figures from bot database logs: registered account count and total question generation count, along with the timestamp of the last update. This response contains no user names, IDs, answers, balances, or files.

3.Why We Use This Data

We use data to:

  • Operate requested features, synthesize quizzes, or transcribe content.
  • Manage your account, credit balances, saved quizzes, and preferences.
  • Execute features such as sharing, favorites, mistake review and practice, gift systems, group quizzes, and challenges when enabled.
  • Verify credit top-up transactions and resolve associated issues.
  • Provide technical and customer support.
  • Detect malfunctions and improve service stability.
  • Protect Abiad and its users from fraud, exploitation, and abuse.
  • Understand aggregate service utilization and improve the platform.

We do not sell your personal data or use it to serve targeted advertising.

4.Uploaded Files and Content

Files, images, recordings, and text you send are processed solely to execute the requested feature, such as synthesizing a quiz or extracting text from an audio recording.

We do not treat uploaded original files as permanent storage, nor do we make original files accessible to other users merely because they were uploaded to Abiad.

Outputs generated by processing, such as synthesized quizzes or transcribed text, may be retained when necessary to run service features such as favorites, sharing, quiz history, or reusing a quiz when identical content is submitted.

In some cases, this may result in another user who uploads identical content receiving an already generated quiz, without granting that user access to your original uploaded file.

5.Deletion of Temporary Files

The service is engineered so that uploaded original files and recordings are treated as temporary files during processing and are automatically deleted once processing is complete.

However, instantaneous deletion without technical exceptions cannot be guaranteed. Delays in temporary file removal may occur in rare instances, such as unexpected technical failures or upstream service disruptions.

When temporary files persist beyond their intended processing window, we take reasonable steps to remove them. We do not intend to retain these files as a permanent archive for users.

6.Third-Party Services

Abiad, like most modern web services, relies on third-party service providers to deliver certain functionalities. These may include messaging platforms (Telegram), cloud hosting, storage and databases, artificial intelligence and file processing engines, error tracking, and analytics.

Certain data may be processed by these services strictly in connection with providing the functions you use and operating Abiad.

The informational website uses GoatCounter to measure page views and link clicks (such as opening the bot). Metrics may include requested URL, referrer, visit timestamp, and general browser, device, and country information. According to GoatCounter documentation, it does not use cookies and does not store IP addresses in the analytics database, employing temporary anonymized processing to distinguish visits. Website click-tracking scripts never transmit chat contents or Telegram usernames.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which involves your browser connecting to Google servers and transmitting standard technical HTTP request headers.

External providers and technologies may evolve over time; data processing by each vendor remains subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

7.Data Retention Period

Retention periods vary based on data type and operational purpose:

  • Temporary Original Files: Intended to be retained only for the duration of processing, then deleted as described above.
  • Quizzes and Generated Content: May be retained as long as required to support Abiad features, such as sharing, favorites, and quiz reuse.
  • Mistake Review & Practice: A temporary copy of incorrect questions and answers linked to your account is retained to enable revision sessions. Under current configuration, these temporary copies expire after 7 days from creation; practice sessions may close earlier. Practice outcomes do not alter original test scores or leaderboard standings.
  • Account, Credit & Activity Data: Retained while your account remains active or as needed to maintain service functionality, credit balances, and usage integrity.
  • Payment & Top-Up Records: Retained as necessary to verify transactions, resolve disputes or errors, and fulfill accounting or regulatory requirements.
  • Technical & Security Logs: Retained for periods deemed reasonable to detect anomalies, protect infrastructure, and investigate potential abuse.

We strive not to retain personal data once practical or legal necessity has ceased.

8.Group Quizzes, Challenges, and Leaderboards

When participating in a group quiz, certain participation information may be visible to group members depending on quiz settings, such as display name, score, or ranking.

Where features provide options to conceal your name or withhold score publication, visibility depends on the option selected and session parameters.

When challenges and face-offs are active, your participation, progress, answers, and scores may be recorded, and details such as name, handle, rank, and match status may be visible to participants or spectators based on game settings. This differs from optional publication on individual quiz leaderboards.

Results whose publication requires your explicit consent are not displayed to other users unless you choose to publish them.

9.Minors

Abiad is an educational service and may be utilized by students under the age of majority.

If a user is a minor, the service should be used with the consent or under the supervision of a parent or legal guardian when legally required.

We do not deliberately collect age data. Parents or guardians with questions or requests concerning a minor's data may contact us directly.

10.Data Protection

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard data and prevent unauthorized access.

However, no Internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security, and we cannot guarantee that technical errors or security incidents will never occur despite reasonable precautions.

11.Your Rights and Data Deletion Requests

You may contact us to request:

  • Access to data associated with your account within our systems.
  • Correction of inaccurate information where technically feasible.
  • Deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Objection to specific processing activities or restriction of processing where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of previously granted consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

Certain records may not be deleted immediately upon request if retention is necessary for security, accounting, dispute resolution, or compliance with legal obligations.

Deleting your Telegram chat with the bot or blocking the bot on Telegram does not automatically delete data stored with the service. To request data deletion, contact us through our official support channels.

12.Disclosure When Necessary

We may disclose specific information when required by a binding legal obligation, or when reasonably necessary to protect the service or users against fraud, abuse, or infringement of legal rights.

13.Amendments to Privacy Policy

We may update this policy as Abiad's operations expand or new features are introduced.

The last updated date is displayed at the top of the page. In the event of material changes significantly affecting how user data is handled, notifications may be broadcast via the bot or official channels where appropriate.
