Download Your Quiz as Word or PDF with Full Answer Keys

Once «Abiad» bot generates your quiz, you can easily download it as a Word or PDF document directly from the results screen or from your saved Favorites. Choose the Student Edition (questions only, without solutions) or the Teacher Edition (questions complete with a "Correct Answer Key" table and explanations) in three professional formatting styles—ready for self-study, printing, review, or preparing practice exam sheets.

This guide covers downloading an existing quiz. If you haven't generated a quiz yet, read the Telegram Quiz Creation Guide or Convert PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions.

Start on Telegram How Does Exporting Work?

How to Download Your Quiz

  1. Generate the Quiz or Open from Favorites

    Exporting is available directly from the scorecard results screen after solving your quiz, as well as anytime from your saved Favorites list.

  2. Select Layout Theme, Edition, and Format

    Supported file formats are Word (docx) and PDF (pdf). Choose from three styling themes: "Clean & Simple", "Modern & Colorful", and "Classic Academic". Next, choose the Student Edition (no solutions) or the Teacher Edition (with answers & explanations).

  3. Receive Your Formatted File

    The bot delivers the document directly inside your Telegram chat. The Teacher Edition includes the complete "Correct Answer Key" table and pedagogical explanations, making it ready to print or distribute as a revision worksheet.

One Quiz, Two Tailored Editions

Select your formatting style first, then specify Student Edition or Teacher Edition and format on the following screen. The Student Edition arrives clean without revealing answers, while the Teacher Edition includes the full answer key and explanations.

When Is Exporting Most Useful?

Self-Study & Active Recall
Print the questions and solve them on paper, then check your answers using the Teacher Edition's correct answer table and explanations.
Classroom Practice & Warmups
Distribute printed worksheets to students or study group members as an engaging in-class quiz.
Teacher Lesson Preparation
Educators and tutors can prepare professional revision worksheets from textbook material and keep an answer key handy.
Blended Learning (Paper + Telegram)
Study groups can solve the quiz on paper and then take the interactive version together, as outlined in Group Quiz on Telegram.

Exporting Lecture Transcripts and Summaries

Word and PDF export is not limited to quizzes alone. Transcripts and AI summaries generated from recorded voice notes or lectures can also be exported to Word or PDF. Find step-by-step instructions and audio limits in our Lecture Transcription Guide.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • Choose the Student Edition if you want to distribute questions without revealing solutions, and Teacher Edition for grading and review.
  • Supported export formats are strictly Word (docx) and PDF (pdf), using the three styles described above.
  • Questions are 4-choice multiple-choice questions synthesized by AI; review the questions and answer table before printing or distributing.
  • Interactive quizzes are solved inside Telegram; there is no quiz runner on this website.
  • Exported files are intended strictly for self-study, classroom practice, and revision. Using the bot during live proctored exams or for academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Please read our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions About Quiz Exports

Where can I download my quiz from?

From the scorecard results screen right after solving the quiz, or from your Favorites list if the quiz is saved there.

What is the difference between the three layout styles?

The available themes are "Clean & Simple", "Modern & Colorful", and "Classic Academic". The Classic Academic layout adds a top header line with spaces for Student Name and Date.

How do math questions appear in exported files?

Math formulas and equations are rendered and inserted into exported files as clear, high-resolution images.

Can I export lecture summaries as well?

Yes. Transcribed audio lecture text and summaries can also be exported to Word or PDF, as explained in our Lecture Transcription Guide.
