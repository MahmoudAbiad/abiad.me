Transcribe Audio Lectures into Text, Summaries, or Quizzes

Send your recorded lecture to Abiad Bot on Telegram as a voice message or audio file, and the bot automatically transcribes it into readable text. Once transcribed, you can read the raw transcript, generate structured academic summaries, export everything into formatted Word or PDF files, or synthesize interactive quizzes to test your comprehension.

This page is dedicated to processing audio recordings. If your study material is already a written document, see our PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions Guide, or view overall steps in our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

How to Transcribe an Audio Lecture

  1. Send the recording to the bot

    Send your lecture as a voice note or audio file directly in the chat with Abiad Bot. If the recording exceeds Telegram's direct upload limits, upload it through the dedicated web upload page. Exact limits are listed in the table below.

  2. Review duration & credit cost, then confirm

    The bot calculates and displays the exact duration of the recording and its credit cost before any deduction, prompting you to confirm that you have permission to process the audio. No credits are charged before your explicit confirmation.

  3. Wait for AI transcription

    Abiad converts the speech to clear, punctuated text. Temporary audio recording files are deleted from cache storage immediately after processing.

  4. Choose your preferred output format

    After transcription, you can receive the raw transcript, request a structured «Academic Summary», export either text or summary to Word or PDF, or generate an interactive quiz from the lecture content.

Credit Calculation & Pre-Processing Confirmation

Audio transcription cost is calculated proportionally based on audio duration in minutes, with the exact credit figure displayed before any deduction. If audio processing is interrupted or truncated, partial credit refunds may be issued. For general credit renewal information, visit the Credits & Usage section on our home page.

You must confirm that you possess the lawful right to upload and process the recording before processing begins, assuming full responsibility for submitted audio in accordance with our Terms of Service. Our Privacy Policy explains how audio files are handled and purged.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • We make no absolute guarantee regarding word error rates or processing duration; accuracy varies with audio quality, background reverberation, speaker distance, and overlapping voices.
  • Officially supported languages are Arabic dialects, standard Arabic, English, and mixed bilingual lectures. Other languages are not actively supported.
  • Audio transcription consumes credits (free welcome balance and periodic refills; not unlimited free usage).
  • Quizzes synthesized from transcribed text are 4-option multiple-choice tests generated by AI and solved inside Telegram (not on this website).
  • Only upload recordings you have explicit authorization to transcribe, such as university lectures approved by your institution. Abiad is designed for independent review and self-study, never for academic dishonesty.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the maximum lecture length that can be transcribed?

Up to 4 hours per recording. File size limits are 20 MB when sent inside Telegram and up to 250 MB via the secure web upload portal.

Will I see the credit cost before my balance is charged?

Yes. The bot calculates the audio duration and required credits, requesting your explicit confirmation before any credits are deducted.

Are audio recordings stored permanently after transcription?

No. Audio files are purged from temporary cache storage immediately following transcription processing, as detailed in our privacy policy.

Does the bot understand regional Arabic dialects and mixed-language lectures?

Yes. The speech model is trained to recognize regional Arabic dialects, English, and bilingual code-switching commonly heard in medical and engineering lectures.
