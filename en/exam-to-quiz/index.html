Convert an Existing Exam into an Interactive Quiz

If you have an existing multiple-choice test in a PDF document, photo, or text message, simply send it to Abiad Bot on Telegram. The bot automatically identifies it as a pre-formatted exam, allowing you to choose between extracting the test with its original answer keys or having AI solve the questions, and then taking it as an interactive quiz inside Telegram itself (not on this website).

This page focuses specifically on digitizing pre-existing questions. If you wish to synthesize new questions from raw study material, refer to our PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions Guide and our Quiz Creation Guide.

How to Convert an Existing Exam

  1. Prepare your exam

    Choose an exam formatted with multiple-choice questions, such as a practice test, past university exam, or revision worksheet. It can be a PDF file, image, or raw text. Specific file limits are outlined in our PDF and Images guides.

  2. Send it to the bot

    Open the chat with Abiad Bot on Telegram and send the file, photo, or text just like any other study material.

  3. Select your answer handling method

    Once the bot recognizes your material as a pre-existing exam, it presents two choices: extract the test with its original answer keys, or let AI solve the questions. The section below clarifies the difference.

  4. Solve the quiz interactively inside Telegram

    Solve the digitized test as an interactive quiz inside the chat. You will receive your final score, percentage, and detailed feedback. If you need a printable copy, check our Word & PDF Export Guide.

Your Existing Exam, Your Preferred Path

When the bot identifies an exam with pre-existing answer keys, it offers the choice to convert it using the source answers or solve it with artificial intelligence. Alternatively, you can choose to synthesize brand new questions from the text, or generate fresh problems following the same mathematical pattern when available.

Original Answer Keys vs. AI-Generated Solutions?

Extract exam and answer keys as-is
The quiz strictly relies on the original answers provided in the source exam. This option is recommended whenever you possess verified official answer keys or teacher solutions.
Let AI solve the questions
Artificial intelligence reads each question and determines the correct answer independently. These solutions may occasionally contain inaccuracies, so compare them against authoritative textbooks or instructor notes before final reliance.

We make no warranty that AI-generated answers are free from errors, as explicitly stated in our Terms of Service regarding AI-generated output.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • We cannot guarantee recognition for every exam layout, flawless extraction of every question string, or exact preservation of source typography and formatting.
  • We make no guarantees regarding character recognition quality on low-resolution scans or photographed pages.
  • The AI-solved option may produce incorrect answers; always review solutions prior to exam preparation.
  • This workflow strictly applies to multiple-choice questions (4 options in Abiad). True/false, short answer, and essay questions are not supported.
  • As with all submitted materials: your original document is never shared with other users, though generated quizzes may be cached centrally and served to users uploading an identical file. Review our Privacy Policy.
  • Usage consumes credits (starting with a free welcome balance and periodic refills; not unlimited free usage).
  • Abiad Bot is strictly intended for independent study and exam preparation; using it during live examinations, for cheating, or to circumvent academic integrity policies is strictly prohibited. Review our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between extracting original answers and having AI solve them?

In extraction mode, the original answers embedded in your source test are preserved exactly as the correct answers. In AI mode, artificial intelligence analyzes the questions and determines the answers on its own.

Can I rely unconditionally on AI-determined answers?

No. You should not rely on AI solutions without verification. They may contain inaccuracies, and comparing them against teacher answer keys or course textbooks is strongly recommended.

What types of exams are compatible with this feature?

Exams formatted with multiple-choice questions delivered as PDF files, photos, or text messages. Free-response, short answer, and true/false tests are not supported.

Can I use this tool to solve an active exam I am currently taking?

No. Our terms of service strictly prohibit using Abiad Bot during live proctored examinations or for academic dishonesty. This feature is intended exclusively for practicing on archived past tests and revision packets.
