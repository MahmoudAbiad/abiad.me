How to Create a Quiz on Telegram from Your Study Material

To create an interactive quiz on Telegram, you need three elements: study material (written text, photos, or recordings), a tool to transform it into questions, and an environment to solve them. The most practical approach is sending the material directly to a bot that generates questions within the chat itself, enabling you to practice without switching between apps.

That is precisely what «Abiad - Smart AI Quiz Maker» provides: a dedicated Telegram bot (@AbiadQuizMakerbot) that converts your submissions into 4-option multiple-choice interactive quizzes. Note that abiad.me is an informational overview of the bot; quizzes are solved inside Telegram, not on this website. You can explore a broader overview on our Home page.

Start on Telegram Read the Steps

Steps to Create Your Quiz

  1. Choose the material you want to be tested on

    Questions are synthesized directly from your submitted content, so send the specific topic or chapter you are studying. Material can be a file, photos, text, or an audio recording—supported formats are detailed in the section below.

  2. Open the bot and submit your content

    Click «Start on Telegram» and send your material into the chat: files as documents, photos as images, and text as a standard message.

  3. Customize your quiz settings

    Select your desired question count (from 1 to 120), difficulty level, and question style according to your subject. You can also provide custom prompts (up to 150 characters). Credits are deducted prior to generation and automatically refunded if a request fails.

  4. Solve the quiz directly in Telegram

    Questions arrive as interactive polls within the chat, each offering four answer choices. You can request helpful hints while answering, and detailed explanations appear immediately once a choice is selected.

  5. Review your results and take action

    The completion screen showcases your score, percentage, and personal best. You can bookmark the quiz for later review, share it with classmates via link (which opens for free without consuming their credits), or export to Word or PDF with student or answer key versions. If you make errors, dedicated review and practice modes appear. If you are a channel or group admin, you can also launch the quiz for your community.

Credits in Brief

Quiz generation uses credits according to the size of the material and the number of questions requested. You start with a free welcome balance, receive periodic refills, and can earn extra credits by inviting classmates. Credits are deducted prior to generation and automatically refunded if a request encounters an issue. For full details, see the Credits & Usage section on our home page.

Don't Stop at the Score: Practice Your Mistakes

Whenever a completed quiz includes incorrect answers, the «Review Mistakes» and «Practice Mistakes» options appear. Review detailed explanations or re-attempt missed questions in a focused follow-up round containing only the questions you got wrong.

Mistake practice reuses your existing questions without generating new ones or consuming additional credits, and it leaves your original quiz score and leaderboard standing unchanged. Skipped questions are not counted as errors, and passing over a question during practice does not mark it as mastered.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • Questions are strictly 4-option multiple-choice format; true/false, fill-in-the-blank, and essay questions are not supported.
  • Quizzes are solved entirely within Telegram; tests cannot be taken on this website.
  • Questions are synthesized with artificial intelligence and may occasionally contain inaccuracies; always review answers before final reliance.
  • Most bot commands run in private direct chat; group quizzes can only be initiated by group or channel administrators.
  • Abiad is designed for independent study, active recall, and revision; using it during live proctored exams or for academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Please consult our Terms of Service.
  • Embedded images inside Word and PowerPoint files are not parsed, and legacy .doc and .ppt formats are not supported.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the service free?

You start with a complimentary welcome balance and receive periodic refills, but generating quizzes consumes credits—usage is not unlimited. You can acquire additional credits by inviting friends or redeeming gift codes. Check the bot's credit store or contact @Abiadd for top-up options.

How many questions can I request in a single quiz?

From 1 up to 120 questions per quiz.

Can I edit a question after generating a quiz?

Yes. The quiz creator or a group administrator can reply with a period (.) to any question poll to modify its question text and answer options.

Can I share the quiz with classmates?

Yes, through a shareable link that any Telegram user can open. Opening and taking a shared quiz does not deduct credits from the recipient.
