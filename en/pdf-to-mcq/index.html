Convert PDF Files into Multiple-Choice Quizzes

Send your PDF file to «Abiad» bot on Telegram, and select your preferred question count and difficulty level. The bot analyzes the file's content and synthesizes multiple-choice questions (MCQs) with four options, accompanied by helpful hints and comprehensive explanations for every question—turning your study material into interactive quizzes solved directly within Telegram.

This guide covers PDF files specifically. For general instructions on creating quizzes from any material, read the Telegram Quiz Creation Guide. If your file is already an existing exam with multiple-choice questions, check out Turn Existing Exams into Interactive Quizzes.

How to Convert a PDF into a Quiz

  1. Send the File to the Bot

    Open a chat with the bot and send your PDF file as a document. If your file exceeds Telegram's direct upload limit, you can easily upload it via our web upload page. Exact file limits are outlined in the table below.

  2. Choose Question Count and Difficulty

    Select anywhere from 1 to 120 questions per quiz, choose the difficulty level (Easy, Medium, Advanced, or Progressive from easy to hard), and customize question types.

  3. Wait for AI Generation

    The bot analyzes the document's content and synthesizes your questions. Credits are deducted prior to generation and automatically refunded if a request fails. If an identical file was previously processed, the bot may offer an existing quiz at a discount, as detailed in the "Source File and Generated Quizzes" section below.

  4. Take the Quiz Inside Telegram

    Receive your interactive questions directly in the chat. You can request smart hints during the quiz, review detailed answer explanations, and receive a final scorecard displaying your score and percentage. If you need a printable version, export the quiz as Word or PDF complete with the correct answer key. Learn more in Export Quizzes to Word or PDF.

Questions Built From Your File: Source Documents & Quiz Sharing

Questions Are Grounded in Your Uploaded Material

Questions are strictly derived from your file's content rather than generic trivia about the topic. However, because questions are synthesized by artificial intelligence, minor inaccuracies may occasionally occur—always review critical answers before relying on them. When generating a new quiz from the same file, the bot actively avoids repeating previously generated questions.

Original Documents vs. Generated Quizzes

Your uploaded PDF file is strictly private and never shared with or accessible to other users. However, quizzes generated from files may be stored centrally. If another user later uploads an identical file, the bot may suggest an existing quiz at a discounted credit cost, with filters available for topic and difficulty. Therefore, generated quizzes are not considered private content. For full legal details, please read our Privacy Policy.

Important Limitations & Guidelines

  • Questions are strictly 4-choice multiple-choice questions (MCQ); true/false, short answer, and essay questions are not supported.
  • Quizzes are solved interactively inside Telegram; they cannot be taken on this website.
  • Questions are generated by AI and may occasionally contain errors; verify critical answers before important exams.
  • Usage consumes credits: users receive a free welcome balance and periodic refills, but usage is not unlimited.
  • Exceptionally long questions may be formatted as plain text, and lengthy options or explanations may be trimmed due to Telegram API limits.
  • The bot is designed solely for self-study and revision; unauthorized use during live examinations or for academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Please review our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions About PDF Quizzes

What are the allowed PDF file size and page count limits?

When sending files directly within Telegram: up to 20 MB and 100 pages. Via the web upload page: up to 100 MB and 150 pages.

Can other users view or access my uploaded PDF file?

No. Your original file is completely private and never accessible to other users. However, the quiz generated from it may be indexed centrally so that if another user uploads the exact same file, the bot can offer the existing quiz at a discount.

Can I generate another, different quiz from the same PDF?

Yes. You can request a fresh quiz from the same file at any time, and the bot avoids repeating questions it previously generated.

What happens to my credits if quiz generation fails?

Credits are deducted prior to generation and are automatically refunded to your account if the request fails.
