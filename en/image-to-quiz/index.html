Turn Lesson Photos into Interactive Quizzes

Send a single photo or an album of textbook pages, lecture slides, or revision notes to «Abiad» bot on Telegram. Choose your preferred question count and difficulty level, and the bot will synthesize 4-choice multiple-choice questions (MCQs) directly from the visual text, complete with smart hints and in-depth explanations for every question—solved right inside Telegram.

This guide specifically covers image uploads. For general instructions on quiz creation across any format, read our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide. If your photos are of an existing exam with multiple-choice questions, check out Turn Existing Exams into Interactive Quizzes.

How to Convert Photos into a Quiz

  1. Gather Your Study Material Photos

    Take clear photos of textbook pages, study summaries, or lesson handouts that you want to test yourself on. Clear lighting and legible text ensure the best results; low-resolution, blurry photos are not guaranteed to be parsed.

  2. Send Them to the Bot

    Send a single image or an album of up to 10 photos directly within Telegram. If you have more than 10 images, upload them via our web upload page. Limits are outlined in the table below.

  3. Choose Question Count & Difficulty

    Select anywhere from 1 to 120 questions per quiz, choose the difficulty level, and customize question types. For math questions, equations and formulas are rendered as clear images inside questions, as described in Math Quizzes with Equations.

  4. Solve the Quiz Inside Telegram

    Receive 4-choice multiple-choice questions directly in your chat. Use the "Smart Hint" button for hints, review comprehensive answer explanations, and track your final score.

Printed Study Handouts

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • We do not guarantee accurate OCR for messy handwriting, distant chalkboard/whiteboard photos, or low-contrast, blurry pictures.
  • Questions are synthesized by AI based on what is extracted from your images, and may occasionally contain inaccuracies; review answers before high-stakes exams.
  • Questions are strictly 4-choice multiple-choice questions (MCQs), solved interactively within Telegram.
  • Images embedded inside Word or PowerPoint files are not read in the document pipeline; send the pages as images instead.
  • Usage consumes credits: users receive a free welcome balance and periodic refills, but usage is not unlimited.
  • The bot is designed solely for self-study and revision; unauthorized use during live examinations or for academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Please read our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions About Image Quizzes

How many photos can I send in a single Telegram album?

Up to 10 photos per album directly in Telegram, with individual photos up to 10 MB.

What should I do if I have more than 10 images?

Upload them via our web upload page: up to 50 images and up to 15 MB per image. Batches larger than 10 photos use parallel processing at an adjusted credit rate.

Can the bot read handwritten notes or whiteboard photos?

We do not guarantee reading handwriting or chalkboard/whiteboard photos. The feature is optimized for clear textbook pages, printed summaries, and study handouts.

Does the bot parse images embedded inside Word or PowerPoint files?

No, those images are not processed in the document pipeline. Photograph or export those pages as images and send them through this image workflow instead.
