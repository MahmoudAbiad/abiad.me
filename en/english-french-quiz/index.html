Create English or French Language Quizzes with Bilingual Options

Send your English or French lesson material to Abiad Bot on Telegram, and select your preferred question category: grammar, reading comprehension, or a comprehensive lesson quiz. You can choose whether questions appear with Arabic translations or strictly in their original language, and solve the quiz interactively inside Telegram itself (not on this website).

This page is dedicated to foreign language learning. For general steps on creating quizzes from any subject, read our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

Start on Telegram How Does It Work?

How to Create a Language Quiz

  1. Send your language material

    Send a file, photos, or text in English or French to the bot on Telegram. Details on file formats in our PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions Guide and our Word & PowerPoint to Quiz Guide.

  2. Select your question category

    For English and French, three distinct styles are available: «Grammar», «Reading Comprehension», and «Comprehensive Lesson Questions», alongside your preferred question count and difficulty level.

  3. Choose your display language

    Select whether questions appear with Arabic translations to aid vocabulary acquisition, or strictly in their original language for immersive practice.

  4. Solve the quiz in Telegram

    You receive 4-option multiple-choice questions within the chat. Each question includes hints and explanatory breakdowns upon answering.

Important Limitations to Keep in Mind

  • The confirmed languages in this specialized track are English and French; other foreign languages are not officially supported.
  • This guide does not promise formal CEFR standardized level placement, spoken pronunciation evaluations, or free-form essay grading.
  • Questions are strictly 4-option multiple-choice tests synthesized from your submitted lesson materials.
  • Questions are generated by AI and may occasionally contain inaccuracies; always review answers before relying on them for examinations.
  • Usage consumes credits (starting with a free welcome balance and periodic refills; not unlimited free usage).
  • Abiad Bot is designed for self-study and grammar review; using it during live examinations or for academic dishonesty is strictly prohibited. Read our Terms of Service.

Frequently Asked Questions

What languages are available for language quizzes?

English and French are currently supported.

What question styles can I choose?

Grammar, Reading Comprehension, or a Comprehensive General Test covering all concepts in the lesson.

Can questions include Arabic translations?

Yes. You can select between displaying questions translated into Arabic or keeping them exclusively in their original language.

Does the bot evaluate spoken accent or pronunciation?

No. Tests are structured as written multiple-choice questions focusing on vocabulary, reading comprehension, and grammar rules.
