Math Quizzes with Formulas & Equations on Telegram

In mathematics and physics quizzes, Abiad Bot renders each question as an image containing complex mathematical formulas, equations, tables, and matrices, paired with lettered answer options (A, B, C, D) inside Telegram's native poll interface. Choose your preferred style between «Problems», «Formulas», or «Theory», and solve quizzes interactively inside Telegram itself (not on this website).

This guide is specifically dedicated to quantitative and math subjects. For general steps on creating quizzes from any material, explore our Telegram Quiz Creation Guide.

Start on Telegram How Does It Work?

How to Generate Math Quizzes

  1. Send your math material

    Send a file, photos, or text from your lesson to the bot on Telegram. Details on file handling are in our PDF to Multiple-Choice Questions Guide, and photo details in our Image to Quiz Guide.

  2. Select your question style

    When the bot identifies mathematical material, it displays options for «Problems from content», «New problems following the same pattern», «Formulas & laws», and «Subject theory». You can also provide custom prompts (up to 150 characters), set question count, and choose difficulty.

  3. Receive your visual question cards

    Each question arrives as a high-resolution image rendering mathematical equations, tables, or matrices, accompanied by lettered options (A, B, C, D) within a native Telegram poll.

  4. Solve the quiz in Telegram

    Tap the letter corresponding to your answer. You can request hints via «Smart Hint», and full explanatory solutions are revealed immediately upon submission.

New Problems Following the Same Pattern

Upload solved textbook examples or homework exercises from your coursework and choose «New problems following the same pattern». The bot synthesizes fresh problems with altered numerical values and variables while preserving the underlying methodology, difficulty, and problem-solving concepts—rather than merely copying original text. Always verify question validity and solutions before high-stakes exams.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do math questions appear as images?

Because Telegram polls do not support LaTeX, fractions, square roots, or matrix typography. Rendering equations into high-clarity images ensures formulas look sharp and readable on all devices.

What question styles can I select for mathematics?

You can choose from: Problems from content, New problems following the same pattern, Formulas & laws, and Subject theory. You can also specify custom prompts up to 150 characters.

How can I correct an equation error in a question?

The quiz creator or a group admin can reply with a dot (.) to the poll to edit it, or use the specialized web editor link provided for math questions.

Does the bot show full step-by-step solution derivations?

We do not guarantee full multi-page mathematical derivations. What we provide is an on-demand «Smart Hint» during solving and a clear explanatory breakdown after selecting an answer.
